G’day, I’m Jesse! 👋

I’m a pastor based in Sydney, Australia. After building Bible Hover, I wanted the exact same instant 0ms hover experience for historic confessions, catechisms, and church standards—so you don’t have to break your flow opening tabs every time a citation like HC Q. 1 or WCF 1.1 pops up.

Confession Hover ships out of the box with all 7 foundational Reformed & Reformed Baptist standards pre-bundled locally: Heidelberg Catechism (with all 52 Lord's Days), Belgic Confession, Canons of Dort (Articles & Rejections of Errors), Westminster Confession of Faith (1647), Westminster Larger & Shorter Catechisms, and the 1689 London Baptist Confession. Running Bible Hover at the same time? Confession Hover uses a distinct deep burgundy accent by default so Scripture citations (blue) and confessional citations (burgundy) never clash on the same page.

Confession Hover is 100% free: no paid tiers, no subscriptions, and definitely no ads or data collection. If you enjoy using it and want to do something for me in return, I don’t need your money—instead, I’d love for you to check out this video of a Bible expert (Wes Huff) tackling tough, honest questions on the Flagrant podcast. I suggest putting it on in the background sometime. It's a masterclass in apologetics.
