Confession Hover — Instant 0ms Confession, Catechism & Creed Previews

Read Reformed & historic Christian theology articles, blog posts, sermons, and study notes without losing your place. Confession Hover detects confessional citations right on the webpage and displays the matching article, Q&A, chapter, or Lord's Day in an instant hover card.

Works seamlessly alongside Bible Hover with a distinct burgundy underline palette and non-interfering DOM scanning.

SMART CITATION DETECTION & CONTEXT SAFETY
• Rich Syntax Support: Roman numerals (`WCF I.i`, `WCF III, 2`), Question/Q&A/Answer shorthand (`HC Q&A 26`, `HC A. 26`), `Paragraph` / `par.` / `para.` (`2LBCF 26, par. 1`), Lord's Day citations (`Lord's Day 12, Q&A 31`), Rejection of Errors (`CD 1.R.3`), ranges (`WCF 1.6-8`), comma lists, and semicolon chains (`WCF 1.1; 2.1`).
• Context-Aware Safeguard: Short ambiguous abbreviations (`BC 33`, `CD 1.7`, `LD 12`, `Dort`, `Heidelberg`, `Belgic`) automatically activate when the surrounding page carries theological/confessional context—preventing false positives on historical dates (`33 BC`), chemistry (`LD50`), geographic distance (`Heidelberg 12 km`), or .NET / Windows Communication Foundation (`WCF 3.5`). Switch freely between Context-Aware, Strict, or Always Match.
• Default Exclusions: Paused automatically on developer docs and code editors (`github.com`, `stackoverflow.com`, `learn.microsoft.com`, Google Docs/Sheets).
